Privacy Policy — FrameForge

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

FrameForge does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All image processing happens entirely within your browser. Your photos, EXIF data, and editing preferences never leave your device.

FrameForge is a Chrome browser extension that adds professional camera-style frames to photos and displays EXIF shooting parameters. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Permissions Explained

storage

Used to save your editor preferences on your device.

Purpose:
Save editor preferences (selected template, export settings, custom text presets)
Data stored:
Template ID, font settings, color values, export format preferences
Location:
Chrome's local storage on your device only
Transmitted:
Nothing is transmitted to any server

unlimitedStorage

Required to temporarily store high-resolution photos (up to 50MB) when passing images between extension pages.

Purpose:
Allow chrome.storage.local to exceed the default 10MB quota for large photos
Data stored:
Photo image data (temporary, cleared after processing)
Location:
Chrome's local storage on your device only
Transmitted:
Nothing is transmitted to any server

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do NOT collect personal information
  • We do NOT track usage analytics
  • We do NOT use cookies or tracking pixels
  • We do NOT sell or share data with third parties
  • We do NOT upload your photos to any server
  • We do NOT require account registration
  • We do NOT use third-party advertising SDKs

Third-Party Services

FrameForge does not use any third-party services. All processing happens entirely within your browser with no external API calls.

Data Security

Since all processing occurs locally in your browser and no data is transmitted (except the optional GPS lookup), there is minimal security risk. Your photos never leave your device.

Children's Privacy

FrameForge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
